# NewsVerify – AI-Based Fake News Detection System

## 👥 Team Name: The Think Tank

---

## 📌 Project Overview
NewsVerify is an AI-powered fake news detection platform designed to help users verify the authenticity of online news articles and content.

It analyzes news using intelligent algorithms and provides credibility insights to fight misinformation.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Fake news spreads rapidly through social media and digital platforms, misleading people and creating social, political, and economic harm.

Users often struggle to differentiate between real and fake information.

---

## 💡 Our Solution
NewsVerify allows users to:
- Paste news text or links
- Analyze credibility using AI
- Get a trust score
- View explanations
- Track previous searches
